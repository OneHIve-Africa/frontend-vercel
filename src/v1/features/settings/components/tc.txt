Terms and Conditions for Investors
Agreement Between One Hive Africa and Investors
1. Introduction
These Terms and Conditions outline the relationship between One Hive Africa ("The Company") and Investors ("The Investor") regarding the investment in beehives, returns, and other benefits. By investing in One Hive Africa’s platform, the Investor agrees to adhere to the following terms and conditions.
2. Investment Overview
2.1 Investment in Beehives
Investors will have the opportunity to invest in beehives that are managed by beekeepers under the guidance of One Hive Africa.
Each investment allows the Investor to receive a share of the honey production profits based on the number of beehives they have invested in.
Investors will have access to their investment dashboard to monitor the performance of their beehives, production levels, and returns.
2.2 Investment Terms
The minimum investment amount and number of hives will be determined by One Hive Africa and may vary from time to time.
Investments are typically made for a period of either 12 months (1 year) up to Ten Years (10 years). The Investor may choose their preferred investment duration at the time of investment.
Returns will be calculated based on the honey produced from the hives invested in during the harvesting period.
3. Return on Investment (ROI)
3.1 Harvest and Profit Share
Honey is typically harvested once or twice per year, depending on the geographical area and seasonal nectar availability.
Investors will receive a share of the profits from the honey harvested from the hives they have invested in. The share will be calculated based on the selling price of the honey at the time of sale, minus operational costs.
Profit shares will be distributed to the Investor’s account within 30 days after the sale of the honey.
3.2 Estimated ROI
Estimated returns will vary based on the productivity of the hives, market demand, and selling price of the honey. One Hive Africa will provide periodic updates on expected yields and market conditions, but returns are not guaranteed and are subject to the inherent risks of agriculture.
3.3 Payout Schedule
For 6-month investments, payouts will occur after the first harvest.
For 12-month investments, payouts will occur after the final harvest within the investment term.
4. Investor Obligations
4.1 Investment Compliance
The Investor agrees to comply with all laws and regulations governing agricultural investments, including but not limited to anti-money laundering regulations and tax obligations in their country of residence.
4.2 Platform Usage
Investors are required to use the One Hive Africa platform responsibly. Access to investment data, reports, and communication will be provided via the platform, and the Investor must maintain up-to-date contact information.
5. Risk Acknowledgment
The Investor acknowledges that agricultural investments, including honey production, are subject to certain risks, such as environmental factors, climate changes, bee health issues, and market fluctuations.
One Hive Africa will make every effort to mitigate risks by providing beekeepers with training, monitoring hive conditions, and ensuring market access for honey sales, but some factors remain beyond the Company’s control.
The Investor understands that there is no guaranteed return on investment and that the value of their investment may fluctuate based on the aforementioned risks.
6. Termination and Withdrawal
6.1 Investment Withdrawal
Investments are locked in for the duration selected by the Investor (6 or 12 months). Early withdrawal is not permitted unless under exceptional circumstances, subject to One Hive Africa’s discretion.
At the end of the investment period, the Investor may choose to reinvest or withdraw their capital and any profits accrued.
6.2 Termination of Agreement
One Hive Africa reserves the right to terminate an Investor’s account if there is a violation of these Terms and Conditions or any unlawful activity detected.
Upon termination, the Investor will receive any unpaid profits and their initial investment minus any penalties or costs incurred by the violation.
7. Sustainability and Impact
7.1 Environmental Impact
Investors will contribute to One Hive Africa’s broader mission of environmental sustainability through the “One Hive, One Tree” initiative, where each beehive established under their investment leads to a tree being planted.
The Investor acknowledges that their investment supports sustainable agricultural practices and contributes to positive environmental and social impacts.
7.2 Social Impact
Part of the profits generated from the investment will go towards supporting rural beekeepers, providing them with income and resources to improve their livelihoods. This aligns with One Hive Africa’s mission to empower farmers and protect the environment.
8. Confidentiality
One Hive Africa is committed to protecting the privacy of its Investors. Personal data, including investment details, will be kept confidential and used solely for purposes related to managing the investment.
The Investor agrees not to share any proprietary or confidential information received from One Hive Africa with third parties without prior consent.
